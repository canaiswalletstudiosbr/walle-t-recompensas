12 prompts de clipes prontos para a timeline

Cole o bloco de formato, depois o bloco da história ou do produto e uma cena por vez. Troque o que está entre [colchetes].

Bloco de formato (cole antes de cada cena)

Vertical 9:16 video, 1080x1920, 24 fps, 4 to 5 seconds, one continuous shot with no cuts inside the clip. Keep the first and last half second calm, with almost no action, so a transition can be placed there. No text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark. Natural textures, fine film grain, no plastic look.

História: continuidade (cole depois do bloco de formato)

Cinematic, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, motivated light. Same character in every shot: [one line: age, hair, clothes]. Same color palette: teal shadows, warm amber highlights.

História, clipe 1 · Abertura (o gancho)

Shot 1: extreme close-up of the character's eyes reflecting a glowing door of light, slow push-in. Sound: low wind and a soft rising hum, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

História, clipe 2 · Contexto

Shot 2: wide shot, the character walks alone across an empty desert at dusk toward a standing door of light, steady tracking shot from behind. Sound: steady footsteps in sand and wind, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

História, clipe 3 · Ação

Shot 3: the character reaches the door and places a hand on its glowing frame, the camera orbits slowly, light flares between the fingers. Sound: low electrical hum, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

História, clipe 4 · Detalhe

Shot 4: macro of fingers touching the light, dust particles floating, rack focus from the hand to the door opening. Sound: soft crackle of light, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

História, clipe 5 · Virada

Shot 5: the character steps through the door and the desert behind turns into a neon city street at night, whip-pan transition. Sound: whoosh then distant city hum, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

História, clipe 6 · Fechamento

Shot 6: the character stands in the neon street looking up at the sky, slow pull-out to a wide shot, light rain. Sound: city ambience and soft rain, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

Anúncio: bloco do produto (cole depois do bloco de formato)

Product film. 50mm macro and 35mm lenses, soft studio light with one warm rim light. The product in every shot: [one line: what it is, color, material, size]. Same palette: [two brand colors]. The label area stays blank, no packaging text. No faces, hands only when stated.

Anúncio, clipe 1 · Revelação

Shot 1: the product emerges from darkness as a soft light sweeps across it, slow push-in. Sound: low swell and one soft click, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

Anúncio, clipe 2 · Textura

Shot 2: macro of the product's surface, light gliding over the material, shallow depth of field. Sound: faint air and a soft friction sound, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

Anúncio, clipe 3 · Uso

Shot 3: a hand picks up the product and turns it toward the camera, smooth gimbal move. Sound: fingers on the material and a soft thud, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

Anúncio, clipe 4 · Detalhe

Shot 4: close-up of the product's key detail (a button, a hinge, a stitch), rack focus from the detail to the whole product. Sound: one precise mechanical click, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

Anúncio, clipe 5 · Contexto

Shot 5: the product on a table in a real setting (choose one: kitchen, desk or bathroom), slow orbit, warm window light. Sound: quiet room tone, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

Anúncio, clipe 6 · Fechamento

Shot 6: the product centered on a clean surface, slow pull-out, the light falls off at the edges. Sound: a soft sustained tone fading out, no voice, no speech, no dialogue, no narration, no music.

Como usar

  1. Cole no gerador de vídeo de IA que você usa, uma cena por vez.
  2. Importe os 6 clipes no FilmCraft pela aba Import.
  3. Corte, junte e exporte em MP4 pela aba Export.
